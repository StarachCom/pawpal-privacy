Privacy Policy for PawPal Health

Effective Date: October 28, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 28, 2026

PawPal Health ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our native iOS mobile application PawPal Health (the "Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal & Pet Data You Provide

When using PawPal Health, you may enter information directly into the Application, including:

  • Pet Information: Names, breeds, birthdates, weights, medication schedules, vaccination logs, and general care records.
  • Emergency Vet Contacts: Names, phone numbers, and addresses of veterinary clinics or emergency services you manually save in the app.

B. Storage & Local Processing

By default, PawPal Health operates locally on your device. All pet care timelines, logs, and reminder data are stored locally on your iOS device or synced through your personal Apple iCloud storage (if enabled on your device). We do not store or transmit your pet's health records to external server databases controlled by us.

C. Purchase & Financial Information

In-App Purchases and subscription payments (such as Lifetime Access or Premium subscriptions) are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details. Apple handles all financial transactions securely in accordance with their privacy policy.

D. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Depending on your iOS settings and consent:

  • App Usage & Analytics: Apple may collect anonymized diagnostic and usage logs (such as app crash reports) to help us improve app performance.
  • Device Identifiers: Basic non-personally identifiable technical information (e.g., iOS version, device model) used to ensure app compatibility and render Home Screen Widgets properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information managed within the Application solely to:

  • Provide core app features, such as pet care tracking, weight logs, and local notification reminders.
  • Render and update Home Screen Widgets on your iOS device.
  • Verify your StoreKit purchase status to grant access to premium features or lifetime tiers.
  • Diagnose technical bugs, crashes, and performance issues.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data or your pet's health data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

We may disclose information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Apple Infrastructure: As necessary to provide iCloud syncing or process in-app transactions via Apple StoreKit.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

4. Data Security & Storage

  • Local Storage: Because your pet data resides locally on your iPhone, the security of your data relies on your device's built-in iOS passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID protection.
  • Backups: If you back up your iOS device via iCloud or Finder/iTunes, your app data will be included according to your system backup settings.

5. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

Because PawPal Health does not require an external user account on our server:

  • Access & Control: You have full control over your data directly within the app. You can add, edit, or delete any pet profiles, records, or emergency contact entries at any time.
  • Data Erasure: To completely delete all data associated with PawPal Health, simply uninstall/delete the Application from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all stored local app data.

6. Children’s Privacy

PawPal Health is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to remove it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
